# Negative concord — and the bare-մի alternative

## The rule: negative indefinites force verb negation

Eastern Armenian is a negative-concord language. Dum-Tragut states
it as an obligation, not a tendency: negative pronouns and
quantifiers (ոչ ոք "nobody", ոչ մեկը "no one", ոչինչ "nothing",
ոչ մի "no (attributive)", ոչ մի տեղ "nowhere", երբեք "never")
**must** co-occur with clausal negation — a negated verb — and this
"multiple, or double, negation is a special feature of Eastern
Armenian and does not occur in Western Armenian." [#1]

> `Ցանկացած փախստականի հարցրեք, ոչ ոք չի ասի, թե վատ է ապրել` —
> "Ask any refugee, nobody will say that he has lived badly." [#2]

> `Այն գտնվում է քաղաքի ծայրամասում, որտեղ ոչ մի աշխատանք չկա` —
> "It is located on the outskirts of the town, where there is no
> work." [#3]

Corpus sweep (sakayan + ghamoyan + dumtragut, 2026-07-25): every
negative-indefinite hit (`ոչ ոք չի/չկար/չխոսեց`, `ոչինչ չկա/չունի`,
`ոչ մի … չի`) has a negated verb; zero counterexamples. So `ոչ մի
երգ կա` is not a stylistic option — it is either an error or reads
as Western Armenian [#1].

## The three-step paradigm

Dum-Tragut's analysis of `ոչ մի` as "the negated indefinite
article" [#4] organises the alternatives: the slot before the noun
can hold nothing, the plain article, or the negated article — and
the verb's `չ-` is constant in all three:

| form | what fills the slot | reading | attestation |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ելք չկար` | — | neutral absence: "there was no way out" | [#8] [#9] [#10] |
| `մի ելք չկար` | article/numeral "one" | scalar: "not (so much as) *one* way out" | [#5] (see caveat below) |
| `ոչ մի ելք չկար` | negated article + concord | categorical: "no way out whatsoever" | [#3] |
| `ո՜չ մի ելք չկար` | + intonation mark | maximal emphasis | [#6] |

All four are truth-conditionally identical (zero exits); what is
graded is rhetorical force. Bare `մի` is **not** a "less strict"
negation — it merely *implicates* the categorical force through the
negated "one" instead of encoding it in the quantifier. (This
scalar characterisation is synthesis-grade — see gaps.)

## Bare մի under negation: two readings

Because `մի` stays a live numeral/article, `մի N չ-V` is ambiguous:

1. **Scalar** — "not (even) one N", the reading forced in
   existential frames (`չկա/չկար`), since a nonexistent referent
   cannot be specific. Song-level example: «Մի ելք չկար ինձ համար»
   (Lav Eli — transcription, see gaps).
2. **Contrastive on oneness** — "not *one* N (but many)": `Մեզ մի
   մայր չի ծնել, բայց ծնել է մի՜ ժողովուրդ` "it was not one mother
   that bore us — a whole people did." [#5]
3. **Referential** — "a certain N … didn't": `մի կառապան չէր
   կարողանում կեր գտնել իր ձիերի համար` "a coachman couldn't find
   feed for his horses." [#7] Available only outside existential
   frames.
