# Մոսկով / Մոսկվա — the doublet and the derivational stem

## The pattern (corpus-cited)

Modern standard Eastern Armenian splits "Moscow" across two stems:

- **Մոսկվա** — the free-standing city name (parnasyan p87 [#2];
  Մոսկվա is also the form throughout sakayan's exercises, e.g. the
  future-tense example in `topics/morphology/future_tenses.md`).
- **մոսկով-** — the stem used for derivation: մոսկովյան "of Moscow"
  (sakayan p431 [#1], parnasyan p87 [#2], tioyan p335 [#4]),
  մերձմոսկովյան "near-Moscow, suburban" (tioyan p368 [#3]). Yerevan's
  Moskovyan street (Մոսկովյան փողոց) carries the same stem; parnasyan
  p245 and p324 mention the street (OCR-garbled lines, not cited as
  sources here).

Standalone **Մոսկով** does **not** occur in any of the five corpus
books (verified by direct grep) — the corpus is uniformly
modern-standard.

## Diachrony (prior, not corpus-cited)

Մոսկով is the older layer of the borrowing (compare Turkish *Moskov*,
European *Moscovia*), later displaced by Մոսկվա, which matches modern
Russian Москва. The derivatives fossilised the old stem — they were
coined when the base form itself was Մոսկով. A side-effect visible in
morphology: Մոսկով declines as a plain consonant-final noun
(Մոսկովի, Մոսկովից), while Մոսկվա needs the -յ- glide
(Մոսկվայի, Մոսկվայից). No corpus source states any of this; it is
inference from the attested doublet plus pre-training prior.

## External attestation: Charents (poetic register — caveat)

User-supplied excerpt attributed to Yeghishe Charents; identified by
the user (2026-07-15) as «Լենինն ու Ալին» (Lenin and Ali), one of the
early-1920s Lenin agit-ballads — the speaker is Ali, a Muslim boatman,
which is why Moscow is measured against Mecca. Text still unverified
against a printed edition. The verse's semantics and Russian
translation (հին as faux-naïf prestige-"ancient", մեծ big↔great,
grabar չի՛ք, the present-tense ապրում է) are analysed in
`research/2026-07-15-charents-lenin-ali-verse-hin-mets.md`:

> Աշխարհի կենտրոնը— Մոսկովն է։
> Մոսկովը Մեկկայից էլ հի՛ն է։
> Մոսկովից մեծ քաղաք— չի՛ք։
> — Քանի որ Մոսկովի միջին
> Ապրում է ինքը— Լենինը։
>
> Տրապիզոնը Մոսկովից հեռու է։
> Տրապիզոնից Մոսկով— …

Մոսկով here is the free noun with a full case paradigm: Մոսկովն է
(nom + article), Մոսկովի (gen), Մոսկովից (abl), bare directional
Տրապիզոնից Մոսկով.

**Caveats.** (a) The text is quoted from the user's memory, not
verified against a printed edition. (b) Charents is a poet and bends
the language to metre and effect — the same excerpt has grabar չի՛ք
"there is none", poetic Մոսկովի միջին "in the middle of Moscow"
(мodern prose: Մոսկվայի մեջ), and երթալ-forms (կերթաս) beside
standard ones (Չես հասնի). So the excerpt proves Մոսկով was
*available* to a 1920s poet, not that it was the ordinary prose form
of the era. The diachronic claim above rests on prior, with this as
consistent-but-weak external support.

Side note for `topics/morphology/accusative_for_locative.md`: the
line «Ու նավով կերթաս նախ Բաթում» has bare directional Բաթում — a
consonant-final Georgian-loan toponym, adjacent to that topic's open
question about the Բաթում stem (goal-of-motion here, not locative, so
it does not settle the question).

## Origin of this entry (nonce coinage — speculative)

The investigation started from **մոսկովապետաստան**, a nonce compound
*invented by the user* (Մոսկով + -ա- + պետ "chief" + -(ա)ստան "land
of" ≈ "Moscowstan / land ruled from Moscow"). It is not attested
anywhere and is recorded here only as the prompt for the stem
question; the word-formation reading of it is speculative by
construction. What the coinage got right, per the corpus: compounds
build on the մոսկով- stem, exactly like մոսկովյան / մերձմոսկովյան.

## Gaps

- No corpus source attests standalone Մոսկով; the doublet's dated
  member rests on the external Charents excerpt (poetic, unverified)
  and prior.
- No corpus source states the diachrony (Մոսկով older, Մոսկվա
  newer) — inference only.
- Tooling: until 2026-07-13, `frequency/query_kb.py` lemmatised
  մոսկով → մոսկ (the -ով suffix strip; kaikki lacks the dated form),
  so KB retrieval for this very word silently degraded. Fixed via
  `HEADWORD_ADDITIONS` in `frequency/build_ours.py`, anchored in
  `frequency/golden_lemmas.tsv` (`validate_lemmas.py --golden`).
