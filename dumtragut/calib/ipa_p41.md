c.	Fricative Voice Assimilation
Consonant clusters with voiced fricative [ʁ] and plosives/affricates may undergo a devoic-
ing and aspirating assimilation: both fricative and plosive or affricates are devoiced, but
only ­affricates are also aspirated,25 e.g. եղբայր ełbayr [jɛχpɑjɾ] “brother”, շաղգամ šałgam
[ʃɑχkɑm] “turnip”, դեղձ dełj [dɛχtsh] “peach”, աղջիկ ałjik [ɑχtʃhik] “girl” etc.
Divergences between Pronunciation and Orthography with Plosives and Affricates
Following these changes caused by assimilation, the following divergences between pro-
nunciation and orthography can be stated:
1.	A voiced bilabial plosive [b] is pronounced as [ph],26 if:
–	it follows a flap [r] [ɾ] in the following words (and their derivations and
compounds): արբել arbel [ɑɾphɛl] “to get drunk”; դարբին darbin [dɑɾphin] “smith”,
երբ erb [jɛɾph] “when”, որբ orb [ʔɔɾph] “orphan”, նուրբ nurb [nuɾph] “fine, delicate”,
սրբել srbel [səɾphɛl] “to clean”, ուրբաթ urbat’ [uɾphɑth] “Friday”, հարբել harbel
­[harphɛl] “to drink, to get drunk”. In all other words written բ b is pronounced [b],
as in բորբ borb [bɔɾb] “brightness, heat”, արբանյակ arbanyak [ɑɾbɑnjɑk] “satellite”,
սերբ serb [sɛɾb] “Serbian”, կարբիդ karbid [kɑɾbid] “carbide” etc.
–	it follows a bilabial nasal [m] in the following words (and their derivations and com-
pounds) համբերել hamberel [hɑmphɛɾɛl] “to be patient”, համբույր hambuyr [hɑmphujɾ]
“kiss”; in all other words it is pronounced [b], as in թամբ t’amb [thɑmb] “saddle” etc.
–	it occurs between two vowels or follows a vowel in the following words (and their deri-
vations and compounds):. իբր ibr [iphɾ] “as, like”, աբխազ abxaz [ɑphχɑz] “Abkhaz”,
շաբաթ šabat’ [ʃɑphɑth] “week, Saturday”, խաբել xabel [χɑphɛl] “to cheat”, հակոբենի
hakobeni [hɑkɔphɛni] “winter cherry (tree)”, Հակոբ Hakob [hɑkɔph] “Hakob”, Գաբրիել
Gabriel [gɑphɾi(j)ɛl] “Gabriel”, Քերոբ k’erob [khɛɾɔph] “K’erob”. In all other words it is pro-
nounced [b], as աբեթ abet’ [ɑbɛth] “tinder”, գուբ gub [gub] “ditch, hollow”, լոբի lobi
[lɔbi] “bean”, հոբելյան hobelyan [hɔbɛljɑn] “jubilee”, Աբել Abel [ɑbɛl] “Abel” etc.
–	it follows the voiced uvular fricative [ʁ], [b] is only pronounced voiceless but not aspi-
rated [p] in the following words (and their derivations and compounds) աղբ ałb [aχp]
“dirt”, աղբյուր ałbyur [ɑχpjuɾ] “spring”, եղբայր ełbayr [jɛχpɑjɾ] “brother”, ողբալ ołbal
[ʔɔχbɑl] “to lament”.
