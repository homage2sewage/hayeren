# Position of էլ particle relative to negated copula

The Armenian particle `էլ` has multiple readings (additive "also,
too"; continuative "anymore"; emphatic "even"). When `էլ` co-occurs
with a negated copula (`չեմ` / `չես` / `չի` / `չենք` / `չեք` /
`չեն`), the **position of `էլ` relative to the negated verbal
complex** picks between two of these readings:

| `էլ` position | sense | gloss | example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **preverbal** (before `չ-V_cop + V`, with or without intervening material) | continuative-negative | "(no) more / anymore" | `էլ չեմ կարող` [#1]; `էլ ոչինչ չեմ կարող անել` [#5] |
| **between copula and verb** (`չ-V_cop + էլ + V`) | emphatic-additive | "not even" | `չի էլ գիտակցում` [#2] |
| **clitic to subject pronoun** (`<pron> էլ + ...`) | additive | "also, too" | `դո՛ւ էլ մի գնա` [#5]; `ես էլ գնա՞մ` (`topics/morphology/aorist_subjunctive_1sg_offer.md`) |

The contrast is positional, not lexical: the same particle in
different slots picks different readings. **Important caveat**:
the slot is not just "adjacent to the copula" — `էլ` can be
separated from the negated verb by an intervening quantifier
(`էլ ոչինչ չեմ կարող անել` [#5]) and still be Pattern A. And
the third row of the table can collide with the first when
sentence-initial subject + `էլ` precedes a negated copula
(`Ես էլ չեմ գնում` — ambiguous between additive and
continuative; see "The ambiguity at the boundary" below).

## Pattern A — preverbal `էլ`: continuative-negative

Preverbal `էլ` plus a negated verb form yields the **"(no)
more / anymore"** reading. The Parnasyan dictionary entries [#1]
are the cleanest pedagogical anchor:

> `էլ չեմ անի` — *Больше не буду.* ("I won't [do it] anymore.")
> `էլ չեմ կարող` — *Больше не могу.* ("I can't anymore.")

The Russian `Больше не + V` translation locks the reading: this is
**not** "I also don't do it" (the additive `էլ`); it's "I no longer
do it" (the continuative). Two further entries at parnasyan p258
extend the pattern to discourse-marker uses (same page, y[6000-6350]):

> `էլ չեմ խոսում այն մասին, որ...` — *Уж не говорю о том, что...*
> `էլ չեմ ասում, որ...` — *Уж не говоря, что...*

"Let me not even talk about..." / "not to mention..." — same
positional pattern, with `էլ` preverbally placed.

The construction also surfaces in narrative prose [#3]:

> Sakayan p416: `հայրը ծերացել է ու էլ չի կարող երկիրը...`
> "Father has aged and can **no longer** [rule] the country..."

### A: `էլ` doesn't have to be adjacent to the negated copula

A common refinement: the preverbal slot isn't strictly "before
the copula" — `էլ` can be separated from the negated verb by an
intervening element and still get the continuative reading.
Parnasyan p258 [#5]:

> `էլ ոչինչ չեմ կարող անել` — *Больше ничего не могу делать.*
> "I can't do anything **anymore**."

Here the indefinite-pronoun `ոչինչ` "nothing" intervenes between
`էլ` and `չեմ կարող անել`. Russian `Больше ничего + не могу
делать` preserves the continuative reading. So the rule is "`էլ`
**preverbally in the clause**, before the negated verbal
complex," not "immediately preceding the copula." Treat the
template `էլ + չ-V_cop + V` as the *prototypical* shape rather
than the only shape.

### A: extension to prohibitive imperative — with a limit

The construction extends from indicative negation to **prohibitive
imperative** [#4]:

> Tioyan p150: `Թե հոգնել ես, էլ մի՛ մնա.`
> "If you're tired, don't stay **anymore**."

Here the prohibitive particle `մի՛` substitutes for `չ-` in
imperative mood; the pre-position rule applies and the
continuative reading is licensed.

**But the rule is not exception-free.** Parnasyan p258 [#5]
witnesses a counter-case:

> `ես չեմ գնում, դո՛ւ էլ մի գնա` — *Я не иду, и ты не ходи.*
> "I'm not going — (and) you too, don't go."

Same surface `էլ + մի՛-V` shape, but with an overt subject pronoun
(`դո՛ւ`) immediately preceding `էլ`. The Russian gloss `и ты не
ходи` locks the **additive** reading ("you *too*"). Here the
subject-clitic position of `էլ` outranks the prohibitive-preverbal
position: when `էլ` can attach to a subject pronoun, it picks the
additive reading.

So `էլ + մի՛-V` is continuative *only when there's no
sentence-internal subject pronoun for `էլ` to clitic to*. With a
subject pronoun, the additive reading wins regardless of the
prohibitive's presence.

## Pattern B — post-copula `էլ`: emphatic-additive

Post-copula `էլ` (between the negated copula and the verb) yields
the **"not even"** reading. The cleanest exemplar [#2]:

> Ghamoyan p90: `հաճախ խոսողը չի էլ գիտակցում, որ ավելորդ բառ
> է կիրառում`
> "Often the speaker doesn't **even realize** he's using a
> redundant word."

Pattern B is **single-exemplar in the corpus surfaced by this
walk** (one Ghamoyan citation; another candidate at parnasyan
p322 `չեմ էլ ունենում` "I don't even [come to] have" supports
the same reading but is OCR-fragmentary). The minimal-pair
substitution test below is therefore a **constructed**
illustration, not a corpus-attested minimal pair:

- *(constructed)* `էլ չի գիտակցում` (Pattern A) ≈ "no longer
  realizes" / "doesn't realize anymore"
- *(corpus)* `չի էլ գիտակցում` (Pattern B) ≈ "doesn't even
  realize"

Ghamoyan's context (criticising redundancy: the speaker is unaware
their word is redundant) requires the **emphatic** reading, not
the continuative — so `չի էլ` is the right slot. The Pattern-A
substitute is the LLM-author's prediction of what the same string
would mean with the order swapped; it isn't independently
corpus-attested, and a careful reader should treat the substitution
test as a hypothesis about the rule, not as direct evidence.

## Why the contrast exists (predicted, no source cite)

A structural / scope-theoretic story for the positional contrast
— not anchored in any of the four source books, presented as
synthesis-grade prediction:

- **Continuative `էլ`** plausibly scopes over the whole
  proposition: "I am not in the state of [doing X] anymore." It
  acts as an aspectual / temporal modifier on the entire
  predicate, and is realised preverbally.
- **Emphatic-additive `էլ`** plausibly scopes over the verb it
  precedes: "[not] even [V]." It picks out the verb as a
  low-likelihood event whose negation is itself remarkable, and
  is realised between the copula and the verb.

The preverbal slot for `էլ` would put it in the topic /
aspectual position (modifying the proposition); the post-copula
slot would put it in the verbal-modifier position (modifying the
verb). Same particle, different syntactic attachment, different
sense. This explanation isn't book-attested in this workspace's
sources — flagged as a candidate for verification against a
generative-syntax treatment of Armenian particles.

## The ambiguity at the boundary — subject-cliticised `էլ`

The third row of the table at the top — clitic-to-pronoun
additive `էլ` ("also, too") — is structurally distinct from the
two copula-relative slots but **collides at the surface** when
subject + `էլ` precedes a negated copula.

Clear additive cases:

- `ես էլ գնա՞մ` "(may) I [go] too?" (cf.
  `topics/morphology/aorist_subjunctive_1sg_offer.md`) — sentence
  has only one possible reading because the verb is affirmative
  subjunctive, ruling out the continuative ("anymore") sense.
- `Դու էլ արի՛ մեզ հետ` (parn. p258) "You too, come with us" —
  affirmative imperative, additive locked in.
- `մի հատ էլ պալիտիկ` "one more politician" (cf.
  `topics/lexicon/code_switching_with_russian.md`) —
  post-quantifier slot, additive.
- `դո՛ւ էլ մի գնա` ↔ *и ты не ходи* (parn. p258 [#5]) — already
  cited above. Subject pronoun cliticises `էլ`; reading is
  additive even though the construction looks like Pattern A
  for the prohibitive imperative.

The ambiguous case:

- `Ես էլ չեմ գնում` — could be (a) additive "I too am not going"
  (subject-clitic reading) or (b) continuative "I'm not going
  anymore" (Pattern A reading, with `Ես` as a separately-placed
  topic). Context and intonation likely disambiguate; the
  prosodic / discourse rule isn't formalised in any of the four
  source books (see gaps).

Practical disambiguation hint: **if there's no overt subject
pronoun, Pattern A and B are unambiguous from position alone.**
The subject-pronoun cases are where intonation does the work.

## Cross-linguistic context

Russian distinguishes the two readings with separate lexemes that
Armenian collapses onto positional variants of `էլ`:

| | English | Russian | Armenian |
|---|---|---|---|
| continuative-negative | "anymore" / "no longer" | `больше не <V>` | `էլ + (X) + չ-V` |
| emphatic-additive | "(not) even" | `даже не <V>` / `и не <V>` | `չ-V + էլ` |

English uses lexically distinct adverbs ("anymore" vs. "even") for
the two senses. Russian uses distinct lexemes (`больше` adverb vs.
`даже` particle). Armenian compresses both into one particle
(`էլ`) whose position carries the disambiguating information.
Speakers of either L1 should expect to need this positional rule
explicitly — it isn't predictable from L1 reflexes.
